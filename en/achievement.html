Notable Solution Award — SCIKATHON 2026

In October 2026, HiHiHaHa Squad was awarded the Notable Solution Award at SCIKATHON 2026. The competition challenged participants to analyze a real enterprise problem in a tight 60-minute sprint and present an actionable strategy strictly on a single A4 page.

Business Challenge

Since the Lunar New Year, the company has faced a persistent shortage of general workers on its production lines. Existing employees have had to work frequent overtime, increasing errors and defective output while forcing the engineering and quality teams to spend more time resolving incidents instead of pursuing long-term plans.

Despite recruiting through job fairs, social media, and flyers, the company’s labor pool remains largely limited to nearby areas. The lack of employee shuttle services for production workers further narrows its reach to potential candidates.

Our Perspective

The team identified this as more than a matter of “hiring more people.” It is about building a sustainable workforce lifecycle:

Attract the right people → help workers reach the workplace → train them to adapt quickly → provide suitable benefits that encourage them to stay.

From there, the team focused on expanding recruitment sources, improving access to the factory, and designing retention policies suited to production workers—helping stabilize staffing, reduce overtime pressure, and control output quality.

Core Insight

The entry requirement is only a high school diploma, and the company provides post-hire training. The main barrier is therefore not finding highly specialized workers, but reaching the right candidates, making it convenient for them to get to work, and giving them compelling reasons to stay for the long term.
