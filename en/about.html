About HiHiHaHa Squad

Four Data Science students from University of Science, VNU-HCM driven by a shared mission to solve practical challenges across hackathon arenas.

The HiHiHaHa Spirit

HiHiHaHa Squad was formed in 2026 by four friends studying Data Science at the University of Science, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City (HCMUS).

To us, hackathons are far more than sprint countdowns — they provide an authentic testing ground to transform data analytics, predictive modeling, and engineering craftsmanship into tangible answers for real-world industry bottlenecks.

The name HiHiHaHa reflects our team’s core attitude: no matter how tough the problem statement or how intense the time limit, we preserve positivity, laughter, and mutual trust to cross the finish line together.
