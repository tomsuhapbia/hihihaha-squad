About HiHiHaHa Squad

A team of four Data Science students from the University of Science, VNU-HCM, driven by a shared mission to solve practical challenges through Hackathons.

The HiHiHaHa Spirit

HiHiHaHa Squad was formed in 2026 by four friends studying Data Science at the University of Science, Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City (HCMUS).

Our team takes part in Hackathons, bringing a shared background in Data Science to real-world problems.

In October 2026, the squad received the Notable Solution Award at SCIKATHON 2026 for a challenge involving production workforce shortages.
